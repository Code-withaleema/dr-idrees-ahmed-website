Meet Dr. Idrees Ahmed

Dr. Idrees Ahmed is a consultant neurosurgeon (MBBS, FCPS Neurosurgery) providing advanced brain, spine and neurovascular care, with a focus on preserving neurological function.

His approach is patient-centered and evidence-based, beginning with a thorough assessment, accurate diagnosis and clear communication. Every plan is individualized, with safety, early complication management and rehabilitation at its core.
